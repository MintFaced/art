2022.

By now MintFace wasn't just minting... it was teaching. The Digital Artist Economy course had students working out what this new economy meant for artists. One of them was Dr Matthew Hirschey... a scientist at Duke University by day.

The culture that year belonged to the 10k project. Ten thousand generated pfps, launched with a nudge: swap your Twitter picture, spread the word. Most flashed and faded. A few became blue chips. And quietly, something less obvious happened... people bonded with their pictures beyond any tradeable value. The pfp became a Web3.0 identity.

That felt like the artwork.

Between April 2021 and April 2022, ten thousand frens followed @mintfaced. Ten thousand faces. Ten thousand small decisions to pay attention.

Matthew wrote the scripts. Every profile picture, gathered and arranged into a single square... one hundred rows, one hundred columns, 4800 pixels a side. A portrait of an audience. The pure diversity of pfp culture in one frame, where the artist is the only person not in it.

Student and teacher, scientist and artist, one work.

**10k Project** ... MintFace x Matthew Hirschey ... minted May 7th, 2022 on Foundation. A single 1/1. Still available.

A framed commemoration edition followed... 1,000 editions for anyone who followed at the time. 998 minted, sitting today with 956 owners. The audience, holding a portrait of itself.

Look closely and you'll find anons, apes, artists, frens... some accounts long gone, frozen at the moment they arrived.

Can you see your Web3.0 identity anon?
