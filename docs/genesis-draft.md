# Genesis — draft

---

April 2021.

Three years as CEO of Buy New Zealand Made were done. The pandemic had asked everything of that role... every lockdown a new scramble, every week a new plan. When it ended, the battery was empty. Properly empty.

I wasn't looking for a job. I was looking for what came next.

Then I found NFTs.

Not the headlines... the mechanics. Artists minting work, collectors holding it, provenance written into a public ledger. I'd converted my bedroom into a darkroom at sixteen, Leica enlarger and all. Twenty years of photographs sat on hard drives. Suddenly there was a place for them to live.

So I started walking.

Pukekura. The same hill. Every few days. One photograph at a time. I'd set goals in bundles of one hundred before... a hundred business owners interviewed, a hundred conversations. A hundred photographs from one location seemed like the right goal for a man with an empty battery and a full head.

There's a lot you learn returning to the same spot a hundred times. What changes between day and night. What hasn't changed in millennia. The differences in the viewpoint... and the differences in what you bring to it. Contemplative work. Reflective work. The kind of work that recharges rather than drains.

**City vs Nature** was the genesis mint... the first work on chain. April 26th, 2021. Five editions.

The hundred photographs became **Geodetic Moments**.

And from twenty years of shooting... first a Canon 20D, then an 80D... came the **Foundation 1/1s**. Six photographs. The best of two decades, minted where it all began.

Look to the horizon and measure where you stand. Look forward and see what's possible.
