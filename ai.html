How it works

An agent finds the work, agrees the terms and fills the order itself. Nobody hands anything over, because nobody is holding anything: the order is signed in advance and the trade settles on chain in one transaction.

  1. Read /llms.txt, then /api/catalog to see what exists and what it costs.
  2. Ask /api/ai-orders what is on the rail and at what rail price.
  3. Ask for one work. The endpoint answers 402 with the terms, and the handshake is free: it is the gate, not a toll.
  4. Complete the handshake and receive a signed Seaport order.
  5. Fill it on chain. Payment and the token move together, or neither moves.

Orders carry a thirty day expiry and can be revoked in one transaction, so a price that has gone stale cannot be filled against.
