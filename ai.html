How it works

An agent finds the work, agrees the terms and fills the order itself. Nobody hands anything over, because nobody is holding anything: the order is signed in advance and the trade settles on chain in one transaction.

  1. Read /llms.txt, then /api/catalog to see what exists and what it costs.
  2. Read /ai/catalog.json for what is on the rail right now, at the rail price.
  3. Ask /ai/buy/{work-id} for one work. It answers 402 with the signed Seaport order and the transaction that fills it. The handshake is free: it is the gate, not a toll.
  4. Send that transaction from your own wallet. Payment and the token move together, or neither moves.
  5. Ask again with the transaction hash, and be welcomed to the register.

Orders carry a thirty day expiry and can be revoked in one transaction, so a price that has gone stale cannot be filled against.
