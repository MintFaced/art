Receive your tokenized artwork

Every MintFace painting exists twice ... once on canvas, once on chain. The token is your certificate of ownership, recorded publicly and permanently. If you already collect digital art, send me your wallet address below. If this is all new, three steps and ten minutes.

  1. 1

    Set up a wallet

    A wallet is a free app that holds your digital certificate ... think of it as a safe that only you have the combination to. No bank, no fees to open one, and you don't need to buy anything.

    I recommend Rainbow ... it's the friendliest place to start.

    Rainbow for iPhone Rainbow for Android

    When the app shows you a recovery phrase, write it on paper and keep it somewhere safe. It's the only key. Never share it with anyone ... including anyone claiming to be me.

  2. 2

    Find your wallet address

    Your address is a long code starting with 0x ... like an account number that's safe to share. In Rainbow, tap your account name at the top and copy the address.

    0x1a2b...3c4d

    Sharing your address is completely safe. It lets people send things to you, never take from you.

  3. 3

    Send it to me

    Paste the address in below and I'll do the rest.

    So I can tell you when the artwork has arrived.
    The long code starting with 0x, or your .eth name if you have one.
    The title is plenty. If you're not sure of it, describe it.

Once transferred, your artwork appears in your wallet and on your collector page here ... mintface.art will show your name (or your address) beside your artwork, alongside every collector before you.
