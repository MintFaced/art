How it's built

One rule, one line

  1. The leaf rule. Corners facing up-left and down-right are rounded. The other two stay sharp. Every square letter turns into a mint leaf... and the shapes still hold when you spin them 180°.
  2. The red line. Fixed at one height across the whole font, right through the crossbars. Type a word and it runs straight through, like it does across a painting.
  3. Built for signs. Wide, heavy, flat-topped caps that read from across the street. Figures share one width so prices and token amounts line up.
  4. Crypto native. Ξ, ₿ and ⟠ drawn to match, a pixel zero for 0x addresses, and Đ and Ł while we're at it.
